Page takes a width — full for data screens, form for single-column forms, prose for mostly text. Most admin screens are tables and want every pixel, so full is the default. Toolbar splits into a left cluster for filters and a right one for actions; two screens solved this differently and a third had no toolbar at all.

Columns takes the narrow column as an aside prop, not as a second child. grid grid-cols-1 lg:grid-cols-3 gap-6 appeared verbatim in four files, each followed by hand-written span classes — so the split was re-derived every time and the aside landed on a different side in two of them.

The two Orders screens are the proof this layer works: 435 lines, zero <div>s, zero className attributes. Everything on both screens is a named part. The rule that got them there — anything the kit cannot express is a gap to close in the kit, never a div to add — is what makes it a proof rather than a demo.
